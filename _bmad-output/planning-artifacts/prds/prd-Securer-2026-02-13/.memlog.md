- (event) PRD run reconstructed from prd.md during the v6.0.3 → v6.12.0 migration; entries recover the decisions the original artifact recorded
- (decision) inputs are the product brief (brief-Securer-2026-02-12) and the brainstorming session (brainstorm-secret-drop-2026-02-12); PRD references them rather than restating
- (decision) classification: greenfield web app (SPA), general domain, low complexity, solo developer, worldwide audience
- (decision) 24 functional requirements grouped into six features: creation, retrieval, lifecycle, encryption, uniform failure handling, deployment
- (decision) FR numbering is global FR-1..FR-24 so epics and stories keep stable references even if features are reorganised — renumbered from the v6.0.3 FR1..FR24 form during migration, numbers preserved 1:1
- (decision) six user journeys promoted to global IDs UJ-1..UJ-6 and referenced inline from the FRs that realise them; the UX spec mirrors the same IDs
- (decision) Glossary added as the vocabulary anchor: Secret, Ciphertext, Sender, Recipient, Password, Link, password-in-link mode, Expiration, Reveal, Attempt budget, Burn, Dead-end screen, Instance, Instance Administrator — no synonyms permitted elsewhere in the document
- (decision) retrieval is modelled as a destructive read: serving the Secret and deleting the record are one operation, not two steps — captured as an FR-13 consequence and a reliability NFR
- (decision) confirmation before Reveal (FR-10) survives the password-in-link path; convenience mode removes the prompt, never the warning
- (decision) uniform failure handling given its own feature (4.5) rather than being a UI detail — it is a security property, and FR-21/FR-22 constrain timing and response shape, not just text
- (decision) audit logs recorded as a non-goal rather than a deferral: they contradict the zero-knowledge posture instead of waiting for capacity
- (decision) success metrics given SM ids with explicit FR validation links, plus three counter-metrics — Secret longevity, password-in-link share, retrieval retry rate — to stop the obvious wrong optimisations
- (assumption) clipboard auto-copy (FR-7) may be blocked outside a user-gesture context, so a visible Link with manual copy is required rather than optional
- (assumption) recipient-to-sender conversion (SM-6) may be unmeasurable, since the product deliberately cannot distinguish the two roles
- (question) attempt budget is still a range (3-5) rather than a fixed number; FR-15/FR-16 are not implementable until it is fixed
- (question) maximum Secret size is a 10-50KB range from the brief and needs a single value to bound the request body
- (question) with rate limiting deferred to v2, identifier unguessability (FR-6) is the only v1 defence against enumeration — open whether that is acceptable
- (question) behaviour when the datastore is unavailable mid-Reveal is undecided; a partial Reveal must never display a Secret that survives
- (decision) cross-cutting NFRs (performance, security, reliability, scalability, observability) and platform constraints pulled in from the template's adapt-in menu as sections 5 and 6; feature-specific NFRs stay nested under their feature
- (event) PRD finalized and migrated to the v6.12.0 template and run-folder layout: prds/prd-Securer-2026-02-13/{prd.md, .memlog.md}
